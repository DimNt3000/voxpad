Privacy and data

Voxpad is a static page. There is no account, no backend, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. Nothing you type is transmitted to the person who published this page.

What is stored on your device

Two entries are written to your browser's local storage, readable only by this site:

  • voxpad:prefs: theme, interface language, speed, pitch, volume, the selected voice and the language filter.
  • voxpad:draft: the text currently in the editor, up to 200,000 characters, so that reloading the page does not lose your work. Emptying the editor with the Clear button deletes it. Clearing site data in your browser settings deletes both entries.

Neither entry is a cookie, so neither is ever attached to a network request. Under the EU ePrivacy rules both are strictly necessary for the feature you asked for, which is why this page ships no consent banner: there is nothing optional to consent to.

The one case where text leaves your device

Speech is produced by your browser, not by this page. Most installed voices run on your own machine. Some browsers also offer higher quality voices that are rendered on the browser vendor's servers, and choosing one of those means the sentence being spoken is sent to that vendor for processing. This happens inside the browser and is governed by the browser vendor's own privacy policy.

Voxpad labels every voice: Runs on this device or Rendered by the browser vendor over the network. Pick a device voice if the text is sensitive, or work offline, where only device voices are available.

Files you open

Imported and dropped files are read in the page with the browser's File API. They are never uploaded. Only the text content ends up in the editor, and from there in the draft entry above.

Hosting

Serving this page produces a standard web server access log at the host, which normally includes your IP address, the time and the requested file. That log is controlled by whoever hosts this copy, not by the page itself.
